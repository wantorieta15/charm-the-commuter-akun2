One line of weather forecast (max 140 characters) for a city that does
not exist: the place is named, it gets weather, and a bored commuter
gets a smile on the way to work.

Entry (129 characters):

> In Crumpleton, light snow at dawn, gone by eight — the gritters salted the pavements, the pigeons, and one very confused postman.

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib) validates the entry (1–140 chars, single
line) and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
